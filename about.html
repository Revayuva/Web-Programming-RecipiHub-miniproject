ABOUT RECIPEHUB

Good food brings people together.

RecipeHub is a smart recipe discovery website built to make everyday cooking simpler and more enjoyable. Browse recipes by category, search by name or ingredient, save your favourites and cook along step by step.

Everything you save, such as favourites, ratings and your shopping list, stays in your own browser. There are no accounts and nothing is sent to a server.

What you can do

Three ideas that keep cooking easy.

1

Discover Recipes

Explore dishes for breakfast, lunch, dinner, snacks and desserts. Search instantly, sort by time or rating, and open any recipe for ingredients and clear steps.

2

Smart Ingredient Finder

Add what is in your kitchen and see which recipes match best. Use strict matching to see only the dishes you can make with exactly what you have.

3

Rate Your Recipes

Give every recipe one to five stars and change your mind whenever you like. Ratings are saved in this browser, so they are yours alone.

Ready to cook something delicious?

Pick a recipe, scale the servings, and follow along in cooking mode.
